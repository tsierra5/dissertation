The two stories I tell here come from my own experiences as a Southern Cheyenne descendant. Our language is considered severely endangered, with an estimated 400 speakers living in Oklahoma as of 2010.6 My family is just one of many in which fluency in the Cheyenne language drops off after a certain generation. Each of the stories I present illustrates how, in the process of learning the Cheyenne language, I come to value the language as a basis for important relationships between myself and my ancestors.
