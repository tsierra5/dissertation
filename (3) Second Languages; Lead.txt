[Preliminary Definition of Revitalization, lead-in to three cases]

To see the first problem with extending Nowak's autonomy-based account to motivate language revitalization, we need to appreciate that one of the defining features of revitalization is the recruitment of people willing to learn an endangered language. 

Expanding the pool of second-language speakers is an essential goal that is required to count as doing language revitalization.3 It is for this reason that the autonomy-based account will not justify revitalization efforts- because it does not motivate learning new languages. I will illustrate this by applying the account to three example cases. Each of the cases corresponds to different kinds of language endangerment, and in each case the autonomy-based account will not generate a reason to revitalize.
